# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **db_git** project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

We encourage participants to cultivate a positive and constructive environment by:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and respect toward other community members.
- Offering and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the broader community.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, experiences, and technical approaches.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include, but are not limited to:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and political or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project maintainer at **kamutalamanisai26@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and treated confidentially.
